## [JWST Confirms First Atmosphere on Rocky Exoplanet in Habitable Zone](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 10.0/10

The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has confirmed the presence of an atmosphere on LHS 1140b, a rocky exoplanet orbiting within the habitable zone of its host star. This marks the first time an atmosphere has been detected on a relatively Earth-like planet in a habitable zone. This discovery is significant because it provides the best opportunity yet to study an atmosphere similar in potential to Earth's around a nearby star. It advances our understanding of planetary habitability and the potential for life beyond our solar system. LHS 1140b is a super-Earth about 5.6 times Earth's mass and 70% larger in radius, located 48 light-years away. It orbits a red dwarf star and was initially thought to be a dense rocky planet, but new data suggests it may be an ocean world with 9-19% of its mass composed of water.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: Exoplanets are planets orbiting stars other than the Sun. Most are detected indirectly via transit photometry, which measures the slight dimming of a star's light when a planet passes in front of it. Transit spectroscopy analyzes the starlight filtered through the planet's atmosphere to determine its composition. LHS 1140b is especially valuable because it transits its star and lies in the habitable zone, the region where liquid water could exist on the surface.

## [TSMC's A14 Process Surpasses Expectations, Yield and Performance Near 90%](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/tsmc-confirms-significant-yield-and-performance-improvements-in-a14-update-strong-interest-from-ai-hpc-and-smartphone-customers) ⭐️ 9.0/10

TSMC announced during its earnings call that its A14 (1.4nm-class) process has achieved approximately 90% of target device performance and ~90% yield on 256 Mb SRAM, both up significantly since April. This breakthrough indicates A14 mass production could begin ahead of the planned 2028 timeline, giving TSMC a competitive edge in advanced semiconductor manufacturing. The process' improvements in power, performance, and area (PPA) will benefit AI accelerators, high-end mobile SoCs, and HPC chips, driving next-generation computing. TSMC's N2 process, its first GAA node, is expected to enter mass production in 2025. A14, as a more advanced node, builds on N2's foundation to achieve further scaling.

Memory tokens enable the model to maintain state across multiple windows, improving long-context consistency.

## [EU AI Act OpenRAG: 933 legally structured chunks with BGE-M3 embeddings](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1uytlac/eu_ai_act_openrag_933_legally_structured_chunks/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A downloadable SQLite corpus of the EU AI Act has been released, consisting of 933 chunks that follow the legal structure (articles, recitals, definitions) rather than arbitrary sliding windows, with each chunk accompanied by a BGE-M3 embedding. The author also provides evaluation results showing structural chunking improves recall and hit rate over baseline sliding-window approaches. This release bridges legal NLP and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) by offering a domain-specific, structurally-aware corpus that improves retrieval accuracy for EU AI Act queries. It enables more reliable legal question-answering and could set a new standard for chunking legal documents in RAG systems. The corpus uses BGE-M3 embeddings normalized to 1024 dimensions, includes exact EUR-Lex links and Article 113 application-date metadata, and stores direct textual classification separately from broader regulatory-regime association. The evaluation used a like-for-like 'whole-unit' baseline, with structural chunking achieving recall@20 of 0.541 vs 0.449 baseline and QA hit@10 of 0.927 vs 0.898 baseline.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Automatic-Forever-63 · Jul 17, 08:18

**Background**: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) enhances large language models by retrieving relevant external information before generating responses. The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is a complex legal document, and effective RAG requires intelligent chunking that respects the document's inherent structure. BGE-M3 is a versatile embedding model supporting dense retrieval, lexical matching, and multi-vector interaction. EUR-Lex is the official online database of EU law.
