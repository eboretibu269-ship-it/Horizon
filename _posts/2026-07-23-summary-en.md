The candidate, designated CD-35 2722 b I, orbits a brown dwarf that is itself part of a binary system, complicating classification using familiar Solar System terms. **Background**: An exomoon is a natural satellite orbiting an exoplanet or other non-stellar extrasolar body. Brown dwarfs are substellar objects between gas giants and stars, with masses between 13 and 80 Jupiter masses, incapable of sustaining hydrogen fusion but capable of deuterium fusion. This candidate's mass (similar to Jupiter) and its host's nature blur the line between a moon and a planet.

PyPI now rejects any new file uploads to releases that are older than 14 days, effective July 22, 2026. This change closes a significant supply chain attack vector where attackers could compromise old releases of popular packages to distribute malware, protecting millions of Python users. The restriction applies to all new files, regardless of whether the release is from the same maintainer or an attacker; it was implemented via a pull request to PyPI's codebase (Warehouse PR #19727).

**Background**: A supply chain attack exploits trust in third-party software or services to infiltrate systems. In the context of PyPI, attackers who compromise publishing tokens or CI/CD workflows could add malicious files to old, trusted releases; this change prevents that by enforcing a 14-day window for file additions per release.

## [China Advances Pure IPv6 Network Plan and IPv6+ with Surveillance Features](https://www.theregister.com/networks/2026/07/22/china-advances-plans-for-national-single-stack-ipv6-network-and-its-own-surveillance-friendly-version-of-the-protocol/5275984) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 21, 2026, China's Cyberspace Administration released a policy mandating a national pure IPv6 single-stack network by 2030, with targets of 950 million active IPv6 users and 42% IPv6 traffic share, while also accelerating development of IPv6+ that embeds content metadata and routing suggestions for surveillance and content control. This plan marks a major shift in internet governance as China pushes for a fully IPv6-only network, potentially enhancing its ability to monitor and control traffic, impacting global internet standards and raising privacy and geopolitical concerns. IPv6+ allows embedding content metadata and routing suggestions in packets; European think tank Mercator Institute for China Studies noted it is particularly attractive for authoritarian regimes for censorship, targeted interception, or additional billing. China previously promoted a similar 'New IP' protocol at the ITU but failed to gain approval.

China has been pursuing greater control over internet infrastructure, and IPv6+ could provide a technical foundation for its internet sovereignty goals.

## [Chinese Team Achieves Cross-Regional Synchronous EEG from Thousands](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5323896905534617) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 22, a Chinese research team announced a new EEG acquisition device that, for the first time globally, achieved synchronous EEG signal collection from over a thousand participants across different geographic regions. This breakthrough enables large-scale neural data collection, which is critical for training neural foundation models and advancing general-purpose brain-computer interface technologies. The device overcame two key challenges: balancing miniaturization with signal precision, and achieving millisecond-level time alignment across multiple devices and locations despite network latency.
