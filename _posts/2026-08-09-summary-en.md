## [Generative Design of Novel Bacteriophages with Genome Language Models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate complete bacteriophage genomes, based on the lytic phage ΦX174. Experimental testing yielded 16 viable phages with substantial evolutionary novelty. This is the first reported generative design of viable whole genomes, demonstrating that genome language models can produce functional biological sequences beyond individual genes or short regulatory elements. It opens new possibilities for AI-driven synthetic biology, phage therapy, and the design of organisms with tailored traits. The generated genomes displayed realistic genetic architectures and desired host tropism, and 16 of them were confirmed viable as phages. This suggests that genome language models can capture complex constraints needed for whole-genome function.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models (gLMs) are large language models trained on DNA sequences rather than natural language, learning the patterns and functional relationships embedded in genomes. Evo 1 and Evo 2, developed by the Arc Institute and collaborators, are open-source foundation models for genomic sequences; Evo 2 was released in March 2026 and is trained on 40 billion parameters with a 1-megabase context length. This work applies such models to the de novo design of complete, viable phage genomes, a scale that had not previously been achieved.

## [AI Wearable Surveillance: Everything You Do Is Being Recorded](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/05/ai-wearable-surveillance-countermeasures/687203/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An Atlantic article examines how AI-powered wearable devices are now being used for continuous recording, highlighting both the technology and emerging countermeasures. The piece discusses the rise of corporate surveillance and the growing need for privacy-protecting tools. As AI wearables like Rewind Pendant and Limitless Pendant become mainstream, individuals face unprecedented levels of recording in daily life. This raises critical questions about consent, corporate power, and privacy boundaries in public spaces, potentially driving demand for legal and technological safeguards. The article references devices such as Rewind Pendant (pre-orders at $59) and Limitless Pendant, which continuously record and transcribe conversations. Countermeasures discussed include ultrasonic microphone jammers that emit inaudible signals to block recordings, with academic studies examining their real-world effectiveness.

hackernews · ike_usawa · Aug 9, 11:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49230477)

**Background**: AI wearables are small, body-worn microphones that record conversations all day and use AI to transcribe and summarize them, marketed as memory aids. Ultrasonic jammers exploit the fact that microphones can detect inaudible high-frequency sound, emitting noise that overrides human speech in recordings. These technologies sit at the center of a broader debate about surveillance capitalism and personal privacy.

## [Envision Launches World's Largest Single AI Data Center in Ulanqab](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group announced that its 'Ulanqab Xinghe Base' officially entered production. The facility, the world's largest single AI computing infrastructure, spans 120,000 square meters, supports million-GPU parallel computing, and has a planned total capacity of 2GW with over 80% green electricity. This marks a milestone in large-scale AI infrastructure deployment: the base claims the world's strongest token output capacity among single data centers, and its green-powered design sets a benchmark for sustainable AI computing. As the first flagship project of Envision's 'Mission Gobi' initiative, it provides a replicable template for domestic compute clusters and reinforces China's East-Data-West-Computing strategy. The base is located in Ulanqab, one of China's eight national computing hub nodes, about 240 km from Beijing with only 4.2 ms data transmission latency and electricity prices roughly 50% lower than in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region. Companies including Huawei, Alibaba, Apple, and Kuaishou have already deployed computing facilities in the area.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 05:06

**Background**: 'East-Data-West-Computing' (东数西算) is a Chinese national project that routes data generated in the eastern regions to data centers in the western regions for processing, computing, and storage. Envision Group, a global green technology company focused on wind power, energy storage, and smart IoT, announced its 'Mission Gobi' plan in June 2026 to build 5GW of green AI computing capacity in Gobi desert regions worldwide by 2030.
